# Training board

Goal: a really great opponent (roadmap objective A), trained in runs from
a pinned worktree (`../zero-vtes-pN`) and fed by the games humans play on
`engine.krcg.org`. Skill: `training`. Docs: DESIGN §5 (the agent), §11.2
"Policy surface and training runs" (every run's command, numbers and
lessons), `data/records/README.md`, the harvest reports
(`data/server-records/reports/`, ignored).

A shrinking list (CLAUDE.md "Harness"; the milestone tokens: roadmap
"Milestones"). Order: the beta's bot first (a run measured and served), then the
loop the beta's games feed (harvest, report, promotion, evaluation sets), then
the levers toward a great opponent on any deck, then speed and layout.

- **A served bot stronger than p14** [M:stronger-bot]: strength has not measurably moved since p10 (DESIGN §11.2 "Where p14 stands"). The impact ledger (DESIGN §11.2, owner 2026-10-25) measured p14 at about 40% fewer A&B a round than the owner (2.13 / 3.03), its turns costing its prey half as much (R −3.37 / −6.06), but A&B rises with winning (the bots' winners 3.6 a round, the others 1.9): a yardstick, not the lever. d16 and p16 did not gain (p16 stopped, owner, 2026-10-30); search on legal views is dropped (owner, 2026-11-02); p17 (human-won starts in the winner's seat, owner 2026-11-02; judged on the nine held-out wins p14 never met, owner 2026-11-03) is served as a trial (owner, 2026-11-06; back to p14 if the human games worsen), VP and wins not above p14's (DESIGN §11.2 "p17"). Done when a run is served by the training skill's "Serve" (step 5) with VP and wins above p14's at one seat against three p14, its A&B a round and its prey's R reported beside them (owner, 2026-11-04). Docs: DESIGN §5, §11.2, the training skill.
- **Bots on any deck, and its weakest recent TWDA decks** [M:stronger-bot]: the served weights' per-deck file (DESIGN §11.2 "Bots on any deck") sets the next run's deck mix and its imitation weight (a human's games on the weakest decks weighted as `--bc-weak-weight` weights the weak precons). Done when the next run's mix cites it. Docs: the training skill, DESIGN §11.2.
- **Paths in the observation**: a vampire's path (`Minion::path`, read live) as a minion-token feature (added to `obs::Version::V4` while no network reads it, else a slot shown unused or a new version: one bump for both, Z-main 2026-11-08), and `Choice::FollowPath` its own choice kind (folded into kind 58 today, a vampire's four paths alike), both taken out of `zvtes_policy::untrained` in `crates/py` and `NetAgent` together. Done when a run trains with them and the server's bot plays it. Docs: DESIGN §5, §11.2.
- **Training memory without capability loss**: measured on d16 (2026-10-25, `footprint` every minute over an iteration): 11–12 GB in rollout (GPU 3.2 GB, CPU heap ~7.5 GB), 14–16 GB in the update (GPU 8.4–9.4 GB, CPU ~6 GB), cycling, no leak — two thirds of the old 24 GB machine (the training machine has 32 GB since 2026-11-08: the baseline is remeasured on it first). The update's extra ~6 GB is mostly the layers' linear and feed-forward activations (rows × tokens × d, 4 layers, policy and critic), which `attention_budget` (rows × tokens² only) does not count; the CPU heap is mostly the MPS backend's per-shape graph cache (DESIGN §11.2's p6 note). The owner chose three changes that leave the gradients identical: (1) activation checkpointing per encoder layer in the update (policy and critic trunks, training only), (2) the teacher's log-probs computed once at rollout and stored with each sample, instead of a teacher forward every epoch, (3) tokens bucketed to 32 instead of 16 (fewer shapes, padding masked); owner: together they must not add more than 10% to training time. Done when an A/B from the same checkpoint and seed (one iteration each, `--teacher` on) shows the same losses within float tolerance, a lower peak footprint (measured the same way), and an iteration time at most 1.10× the baseline's, all recorded in DESIGN §11.2. Docs: DESIGN §11.2 (the memory note), `train/ppo.py`'s Config comments (`attention_budget`).
