# Training board

Goal: a really great opponent (roadmap objective A), trained in runs from
a pinned worktree (`../zero-vtes-pN`) and fed by the games humans play on
`engine.krcg.org`. Skill: `training`. Docs: DESIGN §5 (the agent), §11.2
"Policy surface and training runs" (every run's command, numbers and
lessons), `data/records/README.md`, the harvest reports
(`data/server-records/reports/`, ignored).

A shrinking list (CLAUDE.md "Harness"): one line an ask, its done-condition
and the docs it changes; done is deleted; order is priority; the only
state is `[doing: <session>]`. Order: the beta's bot first (the run under way
finished, measured, served), then the loop the beta's games feed (harvest, report,
promotion, evaluation sets), then the levers toward a great opponent on
any deck, then speed and layout.

- **The lever toward more A&B, for p16** [doing: Z-train]: measured (DESIGN §11.2 "The impact ledger", owner 2026-10-25), p14 makes about 40% fewer A&B a round than the owner (2.13 / 3.03), its turns cost its prey half as much (R −3.37 / −6.06) and its own resource shrinks where the owner's grows. Both measured on the ledger against p14 before p16 trains (owner, 2026-10-25): a larger network distilled from p14 (d 192, 4 layers, against 96 and 2), and decision-time search in the minion phase (the policy's top actions rolled out to the round's end and scored by the critic; first seeing the hidden cards, its upper bound, then over hidden cards re-dealt), p16 then built on what they show, carrying the seats' context (DESIGN §5) and `--bc-games-weight zero-vtes-replay=2`, no shaping. Done when a run is served by the ledger's measure (the training skill's step 5) with its A&B a round above p14's. Docs: DESIGN §5, §11.2, the training skill.
- **Cross-table restraint from the owner's 4-seat games**: the pairing taught it in neither p11 nor p12 and is off from p13 (owner, 2026-10-17); the owner's 4-seat games are its target (at 4 seats, cooperate with the cross-table seat). Once the harvest holds 50 of the owner's cross-table combats (`waste`'s humans group, no seat ousted; 11 in `data/records` on 2026-10-15), compare the owner's cross-table play with the served network's decision by decision and propose the lever it points at. Done when that comparison is in DESIGN §11.2 and the owner has chosen. Docs: DESIGN §11.2.
- **Bots on any deck**: per-deck strength (`decks.rs`) on a sample of kept TWDA decks the runs never seated, then the next run's deck mix from it. Done when the per-deck table is in a run note and the next run's mix cites it. Docs: DESIGN §11.2.
- **Revealed cards in the observation**: since "Revealed cards told to every viewer" (main 15fcfe3, Z-main 2026-10-16) every seat is told a library or crypt card an effect reveals (Mesu Bedshet, Petra Resonance's top cards, Gathii, Andrew Stuart, Recruitment Exercise, revealing searches), but the legal view and `Engine::history` leave them out: a human knows them, the networks never do. Done when the observation carries a public reveal (a new `obs::Version` or a slot shown unused) and a test shows a network seat sees one. Docs: DESIGN §5.
- **Paths in the observation**: a vampire's path (`Minion::path`, read live) as a minion-token feature (a slot shown unused, or a new `obs::Version`), and `Choice::FollowPath` its own choice kind (folded into kind 58 today, a vampire's four paths alike), both taken out of `zvtes_policy::untrained` in `crates/py` and `NetAgent` together. Done when a run trains with them and the server's bot plays it. Docs: DESIGN §5, §11.2.
- **Training memory without capability loss**: measured on d16 (2026-10-25, `footprint` every minute over an iteration): 11–12 GB in rollout (GPU 3.2 GB, CPU heap ~7.5 GB), 14–16 GB in the update (GPU 8.4–9.4 GB, CPU ~6 GB), cycling, no leak — two thirds of the 24 GB machine. The update's extra ~6 GB is mostly the layers' linear and feed-forward activations (rows × tokens × d, 4 layers, policy and critic), which `attention_budget` (rows × tokens² only) does not count; the CPU heap is mostly the MPS backend's per-shape graph cache (DESIGN §11.2's p6 note). The owner chose three changes that leave the gradients identical: (1) activation checkpointing per encoder layer in the update (policy and critic trunks, training only), (2) the teacher's log-probs computed once at rollout and stored with each sample, instead of a teacher forward every epoch, (3) tokens bucketed to 32 instead of 16 (fewer shapes, padding masked); owner: together they must not add more than 10% to training time. Done when an A/B from the same checkpoint and seed (one iteration each, `--teacher` on) shows the same losses within float tolerance, a lower peak footprint (measured the same way), and an iteration time at most 1.10× the baseline's, all recorded in DESIGN §11.2. Docs: DESIGN §11.2 (the memory note), `train/ppo.py`'s Config comments (`attention_budget`).
