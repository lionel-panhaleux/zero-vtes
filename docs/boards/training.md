# Training board

Goal: a really great opponent (roadmap objective A), trained in runs from
a pinned worktree (`../zero-vtes-pN`) and fed by the games humans play on
`engine.krcg.org`. Skill: `training`. Docs: DESIGN §5 (the agent), §11.2
"Policy surface and training runs" (every run's command, numbers and
lessons), `data/records/README.md`, the harvest reports
(`data/server-records/reports/`, ignored).

A shrinking list (CLAUDE.md "Harness"): one line an ask, its done-condition
and the docs it changes; done is deleted; order is priority; the only
state is `[doing: <session>]`. Order: the beta's bot first (the run under way
finished, measured, served), then the loop the beta's games feed (harvest, report,
promotion, evaluation sets), then the levers toward a great opponent on
any deck, then speed and layout.

- **A protocol 2.4.0 human record in `data/records`**, once the harvest holds one (protocol 2.4.0 deployed: decision entries with `window` and `digest`): such records carry a position digest, so `every_record_reads_whole` then catches an engine change that silently moves hidden state (Z-main, 2026-10-19). Done when one is committed there and the test passes. Docs: data/records/README.md.
- **Promotion as a script**: curated harvested games (the owner's, the flagged, human wins worth learning from) copied to `data/records` after a check of what a stranger's record holds. Done when the script promotes a game and refuses one failing the check. Docs: `data/records/README.md`, the training skill (Promotion).
- **The owner's games weighted up in `--bc`**: a per-player weight (today every winning human seat weighs the same). Done when `--bc` takes the weight and a test checks it. Docs: the training skill.
- **The seats' context as an input, for p16**: the owner with Tom (2026-10-22): "a better view of the opponents' action history: what they did, what they're capable of … as part of a context vector", and the position as a context vector for the policy too, not only the critic (owner, 2026-10-23). Per seat, from public information only: the position (`zvtes_py.position_names`, DESIGN §11.2 "The critic against the position": pools, ready and unlocked minions and their blood, the lunge) and a whole-game profile (the history holds 32 public choices, about a turn or two at 4 seats: cards played by type, rushes, blocks attempted and won, bleeds and their sizes, stealth and intercept shown; its ready minions' disciplines, capacity and equipment). Read by the policy and the critic through a zero-initialized layer into the context token (a resumed network plays unchanged until trained); the feature function moves from `crates/py` to the crate the server's bot reads, and the exported `.zvp` carries the layer. After the engine board's "`zvtes-env`, step 2" (it moves `obs.rs`); "Revealed cards in the observation" and "Paths in the observation" in the same observation version if ready. Done when the policy and critic read it, a test shows the policy's reads only the legal view, the server's bot plays a network trained with it, and p16's prepared note in DESIGN §11.2 says it is on. Docs: DESIGN §5, §11.2, the training skill.
